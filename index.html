Equations and model assumptions

The model uses a 4 m lossless line, a 50 Ω characteristic impedance, a matched source, and a propagation speed of 2 × 10⁸ m/s. The incident signal has a 1 V peak in AC mode and a 1 V height in step mode.

Reflection. Γ = (RL − Z0) / (RL + Z0). A short gives Γ = −1, a match gives Γ = 0, and an open gives Γ = +1.

AC. λ = v/f, β = 2π/λ, and VSWR = (1 + |Γ|) / (1 − |Γ|). At distance d from the load, the incident voltage is cos(φ + βd) V and the reflected voltage is Γ cos(φ − βd) V. Their sum is the total voltage.

Step. At position x from the source, the incident step arrives at x/v and the reflected step arrives at (2L − x)/v, where L is the line length. The step reaches the load at 20 ns. The matched source absorbs any reflection when it returns at 40 ns.
